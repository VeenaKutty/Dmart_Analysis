# DMart Sales & Profit Analysis — Excel Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard project built to explore DMart sales, revenue, profit, customer segments, product categories, and regional performance. The workbook combines summary KPIs, charts, slicers, and a receipt-style order view to make business performance easier to understand.

## Project Overview

This project uses Excel to turn retail transaction data into an interactive business reporting experience. Users can filter the reports and review sales trends, customer distribution, category performance, and regional results.

## Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Sales Report
- **KPI cards:** Total Revenue, Total Sales, and Total Profit.
- **Revenue by region:** Compares revenue across East, North, South, and West.
- **Sales by category:** Compares sales across Clothing, Electronics, Grocery, Home Care, and Personal Care.
- **Orders by region:** Shows order counts by region.
- **Interactive filters:** Year and category slicers support focused analysis.

### 2. Customer Report
- **Customer type summary:** Compares Member, New, and Regular customers by total sales, revenue, and profit.
- **Customer count by type:** Displays the distribution of customer types.
- **Customers by city:** Compares customer counts across Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and Pune.
- **Interactive filters:** Year and category slicers help explore customer patterns.

### 3. Receipt / Order View
A receipt-style view presents key details for an individual order, including:
- Order ID
- Sales and profit
- Payment mode
- Order date and delivery date
- Quantity and discount
- Delivery status
- City

## Key Business Questions

- How do total revenue, sales, and profit compare?
- Which regions contribute the most revenue?
- Which product categories generate the highest sales?
- How are orders distributed across regions?
- How do Member, New, and Regular customer groups compare?
- Which cities have the highest customer counts?
- What order-level details are available for review?
- How do results change when filtering by year or category?

## How to Use

1. Download or open the Excel workbook in Microsoft Excel.
2. Open the Sales Report or Customer Report dashboard.
3. Use the Year and Category slicers to filter the report.
4. Review the KPI cards, charts, and customer summaries.
5. Use the receipt-style view to inspect the displayed order details.
